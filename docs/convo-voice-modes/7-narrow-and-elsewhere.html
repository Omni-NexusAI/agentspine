Narrow widths, keyboard, themes and motion

  • Narrow (under 600 px, or a card under 560 px): each option stacks its badge under the name; “Who does what” becomes label-over-value; the facts table becomes one claim per block. Tabs keep 44 px touch targets. Nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Settings never render in the canvas panel. The panel shows only the chip; the gear opens the same modal as today.
  • Keyboard: native radios (Tab into the group, arrows between available modes), native <details>, buttons for everything else. Focus moves to the Hybrid radio after “Always use Hybrid”. No tooltip or title carries meaning (the host rewrites title).
  • Themes: host tokens only (--color-text, --color-border, --color-highlight, --color-error-text, --color-warning-text); no new colours. Every state also has an icon and words, and trust levels differ by border style.
  • Reduced motion: no transitions when a card folds or the mode changes; the research progress ring stops; the diagnostics already slow to 1 Hz.
