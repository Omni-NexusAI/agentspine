6 · A model Convo doesn’t know, and “Research this model”

For a model on no list, Convo says so plainly, shows what was checked on this machine, and offers to research it. The research is a draft: every claim starts unticked, and nothing counts until you tick it and press Keep.

What to look at

  • Unknown is said, not guessed. “Not on Convo’s list” is a row of its own, and every unchecked claim reads “Unknown”.
  • Research asks first and says exactly what leaves the machine (the model name), where it goes, how long it takes and that nothing changes yet.
  • Per-claim confirmation, all unticked. “Keep ticked claims” stays disabled until something is ticked. A claim with no source can’t be kept as a vendor claim.
  • Web text stays text. Quotes are shown as plain, clipped text with the page address written out, never as links or rendered markup.
  • Reduced motion: the progress ring stops turning under prefers-reduced-motion; the words “started 14:02” carry the state.
